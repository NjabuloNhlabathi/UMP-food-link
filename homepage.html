FoodLink

Linking Food. Nourishing Futures.

Creating a smarter and more sustainable campus by turning food waste into opportunity through prediction, redistribution, and analytics.

The Challenge

🍽️ Food Overproduction

Campus cafeterias often prepare more food than students consume, resulting in unnecessary waste.

📉 Lack of Waste Data

Universities struggle to monitor food waste and identify opportunities for improvement.

🎓 Student Food Insecurity

Some students need affordable access to nutritious meals while edible food is being discarded.

Our Solution

🧠 AI Food Demand Predictor

Predicts food demand using historical meal trends and campus activities, helping cafeterias prepare the right amount of food.

🍱 Surplus Food Rescue

Connects surplus meals with students before the food goes to waste.

📊 Campus Waste Intelligence Dashboard

Tracks food production, consumption, rescued meals, and waste reduction statistics.

How FoodLink Works

  1. Students reserve meals or provide demand data.
  2. AI predicts food demand.
  3. Cafeterias prepare food accordingly.
  4. Surplus food is listed on FoodLink.
  5. Students access available meals.
  6. Waste data is tracked and analyzed.

Projected Impact

  • ✅ Reduced food waste on campus
  • ✅ Better food planning
  • ✅ More affordable meals for students
  • ✅ Increased sustainability awareness
  • ✅ Lower cafeteria operating costs
